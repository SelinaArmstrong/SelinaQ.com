Privacy

What this site measures,
and what it does not.

This is a personal site with no accounts, no forms and nothing to sign up for. The only data it gathers is aggregate traffic.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to see how visitors arrive and which pages they read. It sets first-party cookies, including _ga, to tell one browser from another, and records pages viewed, interactions, referral source, and broad browser and device details. IP addresses are anonymised and advertising personalisation is off.

You can block or delete these cookies in your browser, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Google explains how it uses data from sites that use its services.

Email

If you write to selinaq@live.com, that address and the contents of your message are used to reply to you, and for nothing else. They are not added to a mailing list and not shared.

What is not here

No contact form, no advertising or remarketing tags, no social media trackers, no session recording, no heatmaps, no fingerprinting. Fonts and images are served from this domain, so visiting the site does not call out to a third-party CDN.

Questions

Write to selinaq@live.com and say what you would like removed or explained.

  3. 2017 — 2021

    Senior Contract Specialist

    Managed an average of ~500 contracts worth USD 35M+ a year — procurement and supply agreements, master supply agreements (MSAs), equipment purchase agreements, maintenance service agreements, sales contracts, NDAs, purchase orders and amendments. Owned the portfolio’s most complex, highest-value agreements: drafted, redlined and negotiated payment, delivery, warranty, liability and termination terms, and escalated non-standard risk to Legal and management.

    • Proposed and implemented a management-sponsored redesign of the contract execution workflow for 200+ suppliers — identified the ineffective approval steps, coordinated the cross-functional rollout and tracked the results — cutting cycle time 50–75%, from two-to-four weeks to about one week, and eliminating 20+ manual handoffs a day with no added headcount.
    • Caught ~USD 14M (RMB 100M+) in potential off-contract purchasing exposure beyond contract expiration dates, estimated from purchase history, procurement plans and historical spend, and prompted the term corrections that eliminated it before the affected contracts took effect.
    • Led bilingual contracting with Joy Global (now Komatsu Mining), Caterpillar and WesTrac — owned the English-language agreements, negotiated commercial and compliance terms, and worked with Joy Global Inc.’s legal team.
    • Built and maintained a qualification registry and compliance tracker for ~700 suppliers, with documentation standards and an audit trail for onboarding, qualification, insurance and license currency, and contract compliance.
    • Mentored three junior contract administrators and reviewed their contract work before submission.
